# Graveyard Shifts: A Vampire Simulator

A 3/4 top down (slightly tilted) interactive board game (not turned based) where you play a vampire feeding on a town, one house at a time.

You begin the game in the graveyard and exit a crypt, then can walk into town to feed. Upon approaching a house and moving toward the door (ASDW movement with an optional E key press to interact) you enter a minigame where you must chose three correct prompts in a row to be invited into the house. Entering a house  results in that house becoming cursed, and the vampire can stay there during the daytime (or any other cursed house) After three night a house spawns an NPC vampire who also can leave at night and attempt to enter other house (which a randomized effort to be invited in). Over time the whole town could be overrun by vampires.

There is a day/night cycle and being caught outside a house (or graveyard) during the day ends the game (man player) or kills the NPC vampires.

In addition to vampires, clean houses, and cursed houses, there is one house with vampire hunters. During the day they can enter a cursed house and purge it, killing the NPC vampire. A cured house can also release a NCP hunter. So the game becomes a contest between vampires and hunters for who can overtake the town. Hunters must sleep at night but a vampire cannot enter a house occupied by a hunter. A house can hold either a vampire or a hunter at any given time, an encounter between the two is a coin toss for which survives. The vampire can tell which houses have hunters, and hunters can identify cursed houses (but cannot kill the player in the graveyard)
