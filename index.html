Vampire Simulator

A new town every game. Curse it, one house at a time.

How to play

Controls

  • W A S D or the arrow keys move. Walk up to a door and press W or E.
  • Hold Space to fast-forward. P pauses, R restarts, M mutes and Esc opens the settings.

The night

  • The doorstep talk plays out by itself. Your first knock each night is 2 to 1 to get you in; the rest are even.
  • Being invited in is feeding. Any vampire that goes three nights without it starves.
  • A house you enter is cursed. After three nights it raises a vampire of its own.
  • One vampire to a house or crypt. Other vampires may take the crypt, or be home when you need a bed.
  • Nobody can cross the river except by its two bridges.

The day

  • Sunlight kills. By day, shelter in the crypt or inside a cursed house.
  • Each morning every hunter visits one random house and purges it if cursed. Long-cursed houses draw them more.

The hunters

  • The main hunter kills any vampire he finds, you included. No other hunter can kill you.
  • A house purged by the main hunter sends him a new hunter. Converts retire at the second house they visit.
  • A lesser vampire beats a recruit 2 to 1, and recruits die if they find you. Nobody reaches you in the crypt.

Winning

  • Curse every other house and the vampires storm the house of worship.
  • You only die for good with no cursed house left.
