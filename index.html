A new town every game. Curse it, one house at a time.

How to play

Rise and walk

  • W A S D or the arrow keys move. Walk up to a door and press W or E.
  • Drag with the left mouse button to turn and tilt the view; movement follows it. The wheel zooms and a double click resets the view.
  • Hold Space to fast-forward. P pauses, R restarts, M mutes, F toggles full screen, H shows this help and Esc opens the settings.
  • Nobody can cross the river except by its two bridges.

Knock on doors

  • At night, knock on a lit house. The doorstep talk plays out by itself.
  • Your first knock each night is 2 to 1 to get you in; the rest are even. A house that turns you away stays wary until the next night.
  • Being invited in is feeding. Any vampire that goes three nights without it starves.

Curse the town

  • A house you enter is cursed: its door hangs open and it slowly decays. After three nights it raises a vampire of its own.
  • Windows glow red where you shelter, orange where another vampire lives, and yellow in a clean home.
  • One vampire to a house or crypt. Other vampires may take the crypt, or be home when you need a bed.

Hide from the sun and the hunters

  • Sunlight kills. By day, shelter in the crypt or inside a cursed house.
  • Each morning every hunter visits one house and purges it if it is cursed. Long-cursed houses draw them more.
  • The main hunter kills any vampire he finds, you included. No other hunter can kill you, and nobody reaches you in the crypt.
  • A house purged by the main hunter sends him a new hunter. A lesser vampire beats a recruit 2 to 1.

Take the town

  • The cross on the house of worship dims as more of the town is cursed.
  • Curse every other house and the vampires storm the house of worship.
  • If the sun or the main hunter gets you, you rise again in the longest-cursed house. You only die for good with no cursed house left.
